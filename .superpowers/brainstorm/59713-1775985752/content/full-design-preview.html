Case Study Detail — Ledger
Case Study
LEDGER
A simple mobile application and website designed for individuals who want a fast, easy way to track their income and expenses.
Role · UI/UX Design
Timeline · Aug – Oct 2025
Type · Case Study
Overview
Ledger is designed for salarymen, freelancers, gig workers, junior professionals (ages 20–35), and individuals new to personal finance who want low-friction tracking.
The Problem
Many young professionals track money in messy ways. They want a low-effort app to see their monthly balance.
The Goal
Create an MVP income & expense recorder that surfaces key data in a single Overview screen.
Key insight: users want speed (add transaction in under 15 seconds), clear categories, and visual cues for overspending.
Pain Points
01 Entering transactions is slow on mobile
02 Hard to see net position at a glance
03 Too many categories lead to confusion
Usability Study
Study Type
Unmoderated
Participants
5
Length
20-30 min
Overview Dashboard
Users couldn't easily understand spending trends. Charts lacked clear labels.
Add Transaction
Users found it confusing to differentiate between adding income and expenses.
